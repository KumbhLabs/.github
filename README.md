## 📌 About Kumbh Labs

**[Kumbh Labs](https://kumbhlabs.org)** is an open research and development initiative pioneering decentralized AI-agent systems and edge infrastructure for mass gatherings, focused on the **Nashik Simhastha Kumbh Mela 2027**.

During the Kumbh Mela, a temporary city emerges on the banks of the Godavari river for 45 days, housing over **50 million people** speaking more than 20 languages. Kumbh Labs uses this real-world testbed to address population-scale challenges in mobility, multilingual communication, fragmented services, and emergency coordination.

Drawing from foundational decentralized AI research from [Project NANDA](https://projectnanda.org/) and grounded by the [Kumbhathon Innovation Foundation](https://kumbhathon.com/), Kumbh Labs builds open agentic systems so citizens, civic administrations, and local businesses can coordinate seamlessly.

## 🚀 Key Initiatives

### 1. KumbhDoot *(One Pilgrim, One AI Agent)*
A sovereign, on-device, voice-first AI agent companion framework:
- **Multilingual & Voice-First:** Operates across 20+ Indian languages with low-literacy friendly UX.
- **Anticipatory Coordination:** Aggregates anonymized demand signals to help civic administrations transition from reactive crowd control to proactive Government-to-Pilgrim (G2P) routing.
- **Open Standards:** Built on India's digital infrastructure (UPI, open network standards) with offline-resilient edge support.

### 2. Kumbh Commerce
An agentic commerce network connecting visiting pilgrims directly with local Nashik providers:
- **Hyperlocal Discovery:** Enables small vendors, homestays, clinics, food stalls, and auto-rickshaws to be discovered via voice across regional languages.
- **Direct & Commission-Free:** Integrates with ONDC protocols and UPI payment rails for direct Agent-to-Merchant (A2M) and Pilgrim-to-Pilgrim (P2P) interactions.

---

## 🧭 Core Principles

- **Preserving Agency:** Keeping decision-making power directly with the individual.
- **Upholding Dignity:** Accessible regardless of language, literacy, device type, or network quality.
- **Embracing Decentralization:** Resilient coordination without single points of failure.
- **Ensuring Continuity:** Engineering solutions during temporary mega-events that permanently serve municipal civic bodies.
